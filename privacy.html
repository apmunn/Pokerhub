Privacy & Data

PokerHub's current Player Center stores your nickname, bio, theme, saved contacts, clubs, and local notifications in your browser using localStorage. Solo game progress is also stored locally. These are not cloud accounts. You can export or delete Player Center data from Settings. Do not enter passwords, real names, addresses, or other sensitive information in local profile fields. If you use the separately hosted multiplayer beta, the hosting provider processes network connections and the game server temporarily holds room codes, player nicknames, virtual chip balances, and hands in memory. This static page cannot describe every external hosting provider's data practices; review your chosen host's policies. There are no payments, cash prizes, or redeemable chips.
