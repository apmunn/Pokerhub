Learn the rules of popular poker games. Three community-card variants are playable online in the current beta; the others are learning guides, not playable modes yet.

PLAYABLE • 2–6 players

Texas Hold’em

Two private cards per player, five shared cards. Make your best five-card hand using any combination.

PLAYABLE • 2–6 players

Omaha

Four private cards. At showdown you must use exactly two private cards and three community cards. Current beta uses fixed raises rather than full pot-limit betting.

Play Omaha →
PLAYABLE • 2–6 players

Five-Card Omaha

Five private cards and five shared cards. Exactly two private cards and three community cards must form your hand.

RULES GUIDE

Five-Card Draw

Players receive five private cards, then can exchange selected cards during a draw round before showdown.

RULES GUIDE

Seven-Card Stud

No community board: players receive a mix of face-down and face-up cards across several betting rounds.

RULES GUIDE

Razz

A stud variation where the lowest qualifying five-card hand wins.

RULES GUIDE

Omaha Hi-Lo

Omaha with the pot potentially split between the best high hand and a qualifying low hand.

Short Deck

A Hold’em variation using a 36-card deck with ranks 2 through 5 removed. Hand rankings can vary by house rules.

2–7 Triple Draw

A lowball draw game with three opportunities to exchange cards, aiming for the lowest hand under 2–7 rules.
