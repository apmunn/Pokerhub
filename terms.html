Terms & Community Guidelines

PokerHub is a free educational poker practice project using fictional chips only. No deposits, purchases, real-money betting, withdrawals, or prizes are supported. Be respectful of other players. Do not harass others, impersonate people, share private information, exploit game bugs, or use the service to scam anyone. Multiplayer is an experimental beta with possible downtime, bugs, resets, and no guaranteed uptime. The website does not currently provide online accounts or a global ranking service. These are project guidelines, not a substitute for a reviewed legal agreement before a public production launch.
